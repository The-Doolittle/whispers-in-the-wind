What We Do

Two ways to be accompanied

Spiritual Direction

A spiritual director is a privileged witness in the spiritual unfolding of another person. The focus is on the relationship between the “directee” and God, rather than on the relationship between the director and directee.

Schedule Appointment

Spiritual Care

Spiritual care provides triage to those facing various crises and turning points as well as longer term goal oriented sessions. This is also a more interactive form of Spiritual Direction.
